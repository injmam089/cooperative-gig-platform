      🧠 AI-Assisted Workforce Prediction Engine

      The Cooperative Gig Platform calculates workforce demand and capacity shortage scores dynamically from live MySQL database records (133 service requests and 2,020 registered workers) with full explainability.

      1. Multi-Factor Demand Score Formula

      DemandScore = min(100, max(5, round((0.40 × VolumeShare + 0.25 × PendingRatio + 0.20 × TimeMultiplier + 0.15 × UtilizationRatio) × 100)))
      • Volume Share (40%): Trade request share in the target locality.
      • Pending Load (25%): Unfulfilled / active pending requests awaiting dispatch.
      • Time-of-Day Multiplier (20%): Peak surge factors (5 PM - 8 PM peak = 1.25x).
      • Capacity Utilization (15%): Current ratio of booked vs available workers.

      2. Shortage & Activation Formula

      RequiredWorkforce = max(1, round((DemandScore / 100) × LocalityScale))
      Shortage = max(0, RequiredWorkforce - AvailableWorkforce)
      RecommendedActivation = min(Shortage, NearbyStandbyWorkers)
      🛡️ Zero Fabricated Data: Predictions update dynamically in real time from database records without hardcoding.
